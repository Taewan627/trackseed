# Validator v1.3 exploration (EXPLORATORY)

Status: exploratory. This is not a protocol and makes no prediction; it records how two defaults of validator v1.3 were chosen and what they do to the probes of the third red team (RT3, reviewer R2, issues R2-1 and R2-2). Rule definitions and the probe outcomes are in [validator.md](validator.md), "Absolute per-vertex rule and sampling diagnostic". Script: `scripts/validator-v1.3-exploration.mjs` (needs `npm run build:cli`; about 6 minutes on the 2-vCPU machine, `--probes-only` runs the probe table alone in a second). The numbers below are the output of that script.

## Question

The relative local-kink rule (flag iff theta_i > max(12 degrees, 3 x the largest turn at i +- 2..3)) cannot see defects that are as sharp as their own neighbourhood: coarse polygons, a uniform sawtooth, paired spikes. Two additions were requested: an absolute per-vertex limit `sharpVertexDegrees` (error `SHARP_VERTEX`) and a sampling diagnostic (warning `UNDERSAMPLED`). Both need numbers: T_abs, and how many vertices over which turn make a polyline undersampled.

## Data

Clean tracks generated by TrackSeed, none of them created for this evaluation (the grids are those of experiment E6, `experiments/e6/lib.mjs`):

- **DEV** (selection): the E6 DEV seeds (20), negative sets N1 (pattern modes: right-angle, hairpin, S-curve; curves 3, 6, 10; straight 0, 0.3, 0.6; width 20, 40, 80; size 1, 2, 3; height 0, 200) and N2 (Auto, straight 0), legacy join as in E6 (`autoJoin: 'legacy'`): 9,718 and 1,080 tracks.
- **Confirmation** (not used for the choice): the E6 held-out seeds (40, legacy join, 21,596 tracks); the E2 seeds (50) with v1.2 defaults, that is the default tangent join (26,996 N1/N2 tracks) and Auto with straight 0.3 and 0.6, the case the tangent join was built for (900 tracks).
- **N3** (analytic circles of 12 to 384 vertices, ellipses and stadiums, E6): 60 tracks; they are not generated tracks and are reported only as the coarsest "natural" polylines.

Measured per track: the largest heading change at any vertex (seam included) and the share of vertices whose heading change exceeds 12, 8 and 6 degrees.

Reading: no clean generated track turns by more than 13.85 degrees at a vertex, except Auto with straight > 0 and the tangent join, whose largest turn is 17.43 degrees (81 of 900 tracks above 14 degrees, none above 20). (The E6 documents quote 13.6 degrees as the largest clean turn; that figure comes from the pilot measurement in the E6 protocol. Over the full DEV grid the maximum is 13.83 degrees, measured here, so the 12 degree floor of the relative rule is below the clean maximum, as docs/validator.md already says: the ratio term does the work there.) The share of vertices above 12 degrees is at most 2.69 % on pattern and Auto straight-0 tracks and at most 4.44 % on Auto with straight > 0. The N3 row is the synthetic stress set: its 12-vertex circles turn 30 degrees per vertex, its 24-vertex circles 15 degrees.

## Choice

- **`sharpVertexDegrees` = 30.** Margin above the clean data: 30 / 17.43 = 1.72 (and 30 / 13.85 = 2.17 for the pattern modes); no clean generated track is flagged (0 of 60,290 turn by more than 20 degrees; the largest turn is 17.43 degrees). Margin below the defect classes of RT3: the weakest probe is the sawtooth of alternating +-0.5 at width 10 (35.5 degrees, ratio 1.18), then the regular octagon (45 degrees, 1.5). The N3 circles of 12 vertices sit exactly on the limit: their turn is 30 degrees up to rounding (30.00000000000002 in the double-precision computation), so the strict test flags some of their vertices and not others by rounding noise, not by margin, and the verdict for a regular 12-gon carries no meaning; circles of 13 or more vertices (27.7 degrees or less) pass and circles of 11 or fewer (32.7 degrees or more) fail by margin. 30 degrees is also the size of the must-detect defect of E6 (a design choice there as well). It is a choice, not a derived constant: a smaller value would eat into the clean margin (17.43 degrees), a value above 35.5 would accept the sawtooth at +-0.5.
- **UNDERSAMPLED: more than `undersampledShare` = 10 % of the n vertices turn by more than `undersampledTurnDegrees` = 12 degrees.** The 12 degrees are the floor of the relative rule and the turn of a vertex on an arc sampled at s = 0.2 R (docs/validator.md, "Sampling requirement" (1)), so a polyline that satisfies the documented requirement has no such vertex on its arcs. Spacing against implied radius is therefore not a second test: the turn of a vertex on an arc is 2 asin(s / 2R) and is the observable form of s / R. The share (rather than "any vertex") keeps isolated defects, which are the business of `LOCAL_KINK` and `SHARP_VERTEX`, from being reported as a sampling problem, and lets a clean track with a few tight corners pass. 10 % is 2.25 times the largest share found on clean generated tracks (4.44 %) and 3.7 times that on pattern modes (2.69 %); every polygon and circle of fewer than 30 vertices has share 100 %. The report carries `share`, `verticesOverTurn`, `maxTurnDegrees`, `medianSpacing`, `maxSpacing` and `width` so that batch studies can filter on them.

Both checks are on by default in the default mode and can be disabled (`sharpVertexDegrees: Infinity` or `--no-sharp-vertex`; `undersampledCheck: false` or `--no-undersampled-check`).

## Limits of this exploration

- Both defaults were chosen on TrackSeed's own generator, which is the home-advantage objection of R2-5 in a smaller form: clean data other than TrackSeed's (the real circuits and the third-party generators of E9) was not used to select anything and is not evaluated here. The margins say how far the defaults are from TrackSeed's clean tracks, nothing about other sources. Real circuits sampled coarsely (E9) are expected to be flagged `UNDERSAMPLED` and sometimes `SHARP_VERTEX`; the committed E9 results were produced without the new checks and are reproduced with them switched off (experiments/_v12-validator.mjs).
- The probe table was read before the threshold was fixed, so the upper side of the 30-degree choice is informed by the probes; it is not a held-out test of the rule. The probes are a few shapes, not a distribution of defects.
- The confirmation sets use the same generator as the selection set. The grids are balanced factorials, not a natural distribution of parameters.
- `SHARP_VERTEX` and `UNDERSAMPLED` judge the polyline as given. After `--resample-smooth` the spline rounds the corners of the coarse polygons (the 4-, 5- and 8-gon pass with a largest turn of 1.4, 0.9 and 0.5 degrees at S = 3), so both arms (raw and smoothed) must be reported and the raw arm carries the new checks.
- The share is a heuristic for "coarser than the documented requirement"; it does not estimate the curve that was meant, and it does not detect an undersampled polyline whose curved part is under 10 % of its vertices.
