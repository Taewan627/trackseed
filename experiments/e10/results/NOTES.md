## Caveats that matter for interpretation
- **30 degrees was chosen after the RT3 probes and on TrackSeed's tracks** (PROTOCOL section 0). The 100 % on the must classes under v1.3 is true by construction (P2). What E10 adds is the description of the classes under four ablations, the masking boundary (gap 4/5), the amplitude sweeps on grey and sub instances (where v1.3 adds nothing), the polygons accepted at 13 to 16 vertices, and the decimation behaviour. It is not an independent confirmation of the threshold, and it says nothing about third-party tracks.
- **Intervals are narrow because the grid is balanced.** The bases are a balanced factorial; every seed has the same cells, so the seed-clustered bootstrap intervals are often very narrow (for example [50.0, 50.0] for the warning share on N1). They describe the sensitivity of the pooled rate to the choice of the 40 seeds, not uncertainty about a population of tracks (R2-9). Degenerate cells carry the seed-level Clopper-Pearson bound (0 of 40 seeds: upper 8.8 %; 40 of 40: lower 91.2 %), which bounds the share of seeds with an exception, not of instances.
- The kink-family false-positive rate is on clean TrackSeed tracks only. The generator's own errors (folds, crossings; 10.2 % of Auto straight-0 tracks and 17.7 % of N4 tracks) are outside what the kink rules judge and are kept out of the false-positive definition.
- 4 N1 cases failed to generate (hairpin exception), as in E6 (19436 generated).
- Rates in the (a) and (b) classes mix three width levels and two mode types; the stratified tables (width, mode type) are in `SUMMARY.md` and `SUMMARY.json`.
