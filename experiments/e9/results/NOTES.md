## Caveats that matter for interpretation
- **juangallostra: quantisation dominates.** Its polylines are integer pixel positions at 2-4 px spacing (236 of 998 tracks contain duplicate vertices that the built-in removal drops, 626 vertices in total). Every raw track fails `LOCAL_KINK` (quantisation kinks) and `EDGE_FOLD` / `SELF_INTERSECTION_EDGE` (the 40 px road against its tight turns; `MIN_TURN_RADIUS` warns on all 998). Its Q2 values (mean |kappa-hat| about 18, about 450 turns) are mostly noise from the integer grid that survives even the norm-720 smoothing; they are not a statement about its track shapes. Smoothing at 10 px rescues 12.8 % only: here the width (40 px) and the geometry, not just the sampling, make the tracks invalid by the validator's rules.
- **The validator is planar.** The one TUMFTM circuit failing with centreline self-intersection codes (Suzuka) is the figure-eight crossover, which is grade-separated in reality; the file has no heights. A planar `SELF_INTERSECTION_CENTERLINE` on it is a limitation of the input (no height), not a defect of the circuit.
- **TUMFTM single width.** One width per circuit (median); the minimum-width run does not change the raw count (15 both) but changes the smooth count (20 -> 24).
- **Spacing is confounded with source type.** Real circuits are sampled at 5 m (TUMFTM) or 12-38 m (bacinger) with roads 9-16 m wide; the sampling guideline of `docs/validator.md` (s <= 0.2 R_min) is a plausible reason for the `LOCAL_KINK` failures of tight corners, but E9 did not test that explanation; only the raw/smooth contrast was measured.
- **drift-school** (supplementary): 100 % raw ok in all five stages; its tracks are nearly circular (mean |kappa-hat| 1.09-1.16 against 4.4 for real circuits), so it is a narrow-range generator; its self-filtering validator makes the high ok rate expected. 3-8 tracks per stage 0-2 turn invalid after smoothing.
- **Real vs generated, Q2.** All five primary generator sources differ from the 25 circuits in at least 4 of 6 normalised metrics (threshold 0.78-0.81 SD). The comparison is of 1000-track samples against a 25-circuit reference (the split baseline gives the noise allowance, median d of a random 12/13 split about 0.41-0.52).
- One track per seed, default parameters of each generator, one machine. Generation failures (AMZ 79, juangallostra 2) are not retried and are excluded from the ok rates but shown in the "of attempted" column.
- The SVG figure is large (2 MB) because every track is a point; the PNG is the paper-sized version.
