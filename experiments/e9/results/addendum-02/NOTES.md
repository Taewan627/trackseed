## What the numbers say (and do not say)
- The diagnostic separates the sources into three kinds under one rule set (raw arm): sources with no UNDERSAMPLED and no SHARP_VERTEX (TrackSeed, E5, AMZ, drift-school; ok rates are decided by fold and crossing errors or by nothing), sources whose polyline is coarse for their own geometry (CarRacing, track_gen, bacinger: UNDERSAMPLED present in 55 to 100 % of tracks, ok rates change strongly after smoothing), and one source whose input is a quantised grid (juangallostra). TUMFTM is in a fourth position: no UNDERSAMPLED but 6 of 25 SHARP_VERTEX at 5 m spacing, which are the coarse apexes of tight corners, not a sampling pattern the share rule sees.
- TUMFTM raw ok falls from 15 (v1.2) to 13 of 25 (v1.3); cs25 stays at 20 of 25, and at the minimum width cs25 passes 24 of 25. bacinger raw falls from 20 to 3 of 40 (36 tracks with SHARP_VERTEX, 40 with UNDERSAMPLED): its 12 to 38 m spacing is far below the sampling requirement for the width assumed (12 m), so the raw bacinger result is a sampling statement; cs25 gives 32 of 40, as in E9.
- Common-rule smoothing at 0.25 x W does not make every source adequately sampled: CarRacing (41.4 %), juangallostra (54.4 %) and drift-school stage 0 (3.7 %) still carry UNDERSAMPLED after cs25; at cs10 only juangallostra does (quantisation noise grows relative to the spacing). cs10 also turns folds and crossings (the generators' own width-versus-curvature failures) into the dominant error for track_gen and CarRacing (cs10 CarRacing 99.9 % ok, track_gen 77.7 %).
- None of this ranks the sources. AMZ's 99.2 % is over 921 produced tracks (91.4 % of the 1000 attempted); AMZ, E5, drift-school, juangallostra and the CarRacing family filter or construct their own tracks, TrackSeed does not (both denominators and the filtering note are in the table).

## Caveats
- Home advantage: v1.3 was calibrated on TrackSeed (protocol section 0, 1); TrackSeed passing every check is therefore not evidence of its quality.
- One track per seed; widths are the authors' constants (only TUMFTM has a width run); cs25 and cs10 change the validated geometry and can hide spikes (R2-3).
- A width sensitivity for the generators other than TUMFTM (R2-8) and a drivability ground truth (R2-4 d) are not part of this addendum.
- juangallostra is not a headline number (integer grid). The q column of the quantisation table is computed for every source with a half-unit grid; it is meaningful only for juangallostra, the one source whose coordinates are integers (the other rows are shown for scale and carry no quantisation claim).
